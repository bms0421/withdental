TREATMENTS

What we treat

From conservative tooth-saving restorations to full-mouth rehabilitation and orthodontics, under one philosophy.

Biomimetic Restorations

Adhesive overlays, resin build-ups, and protection of cracked teeth with minimal tooth reduction.

Esthetic Prosthodontics

Natural-looking front-tooth restorations, minimal-prep veneers, resin veneers, and black-triangle treatment.

Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

Restoring a stable, long-lasting bite, including re-treatment of old or ill-fitting restorations.

Periodontal Plastic Surgery

Root coverage, gingivoplasty, and gummy-smile correction.

Crowns, Bridges, Dentures & All-on-X

Restoring lost or badly damaged teeth in both function and appearance, including All-on-X full-arch implant prostheses.

Top-Down Implants

The final tooth position and shape are planned first, then the implant is placed accordingly.

Orthodontics

Braces (metal, Clippy-C), Invisalign, limited orthodontics, pediatric and surgical orthodontics, and re-treatment.

Interdisciplinary Care

Orthodontics before prosthetics to reduce tooth reduction, planned together by both specialists.

HOW IT WORKS

Your visit, step by step

Your first visit is for examination and consultation only. Treatment starts at your next appointment, once you have had time to decide.

  1. 1
    BEFORE YOUR VISIT

    Book by Email or KakaoTalk

    Tell us briefly what is bothering you and your preferred dates. We will confirm your appointment.

  2. 2
    FIRST VISIT

    Registration

    Fill out our medical history form, prepared in English. Let us know if your insurer needs specific documents. We will also check whether you are covered by Korean National Health Insurance.

  3. 3
    FIRST VISIT

    Clinical Examination

    A full clinical examination, including X-rays.

  4. 4
    FIRST VISIT

    Consultation in English

    Based on your main concern, Dr. Bae or Dr. Kang examines you and takes high-resolution photographs, then explains treatment options and costs in English.

  5. 5
    NEXT VISITS

    Treatment

    Treatment begins from your next visit, booked after the first-day consultation.

  6. 6
    AFTER TREATMENT

    Documents

    We issue what you need: insurance claim forms, tax-related documents, itemized receipts, and medical records for immigration if required.
